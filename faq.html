Ask a question
What does Ah mean?+

Ah stands for ampere-hour. It describes battery capacity under specified conditions. Backup time also depends on your load and inverter.

What is the difference between C10 and C20?+

C10 and C20 are capacity rating formats based on different discharge durations. Ask the team which model is appropriate for your inverter.

Which capacity should I choose?+

The best choice depends on your inverter, the appliances you use and desired backup duration. Share these details in the enquiry form.

How do I request a callback? +

Open Enquiry, select Request callback and share your phone number. Our team can contact you.

How do I choose a battery capacity? +

Consider your appliance load, inverter and desired backup time. Our team can help you choose.
